Pirates on Florida's Emerald Coast

Pirates of the White Sands is a Panama City, Florida-based pirate crew appearing throughout Panama City, Panama City Beach and the surrounding Florida Panhandle. You may find the crew at festivals, charity events, parades, historical demonstrations, waterfront celebrations and family-friendly community gatherings.

Whether you're a local looking for things to do in Panama City, a visitor searching for things to do in Panama City Beach, or a pirate fan hunting for the next pirate festival in Florida, our calendar is the best place to see where we'll drop anchor next.
