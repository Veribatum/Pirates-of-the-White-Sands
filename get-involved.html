Good People. Rowdy Times.

Join the Crew. Lend a Hand.

Pirates of the White Sands is built around friendship, community, charity, and a shared love of pirate culture. You do not need a perfect costume, a pirate résumé, or years of reenactment experience to get involved.

What matters is showing up, pitching in, learning from the crew, and being willing to help when there is work to do — whether that means supporting a charity event, helping with setup and teardown, greeting families, or carrying half the damn ship.
