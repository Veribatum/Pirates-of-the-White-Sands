Meet the Krewe

Who We ARRR!

Pirates of the White Sands is a nonprofit pirate krewe based in Panama City, Florida. We bring together costuming, community events, charity work, and a shared love of pirate culture.

You’ll find us supporting local causes, appearing at festivals and special events, and helping bring a little adventure to the Gulf Coast.

Our Biggest Adventure

Pirates of the High Seas & Renaissance Fest

From breakfast with pirates to parades in the streets, the weekend is packed with adventure for the whole crew.

Pirates of the White Sands brings living history, sword fighting, educational demonstrations, a historically inspired encampment, and our ship float to the festivities in Panama City Beach.

Our Home Port

Grace O'Malley's

Every good krewe needs a home port. For Pirates of the White Sands, that place is Grace O'Malley's in Panama City.

It's where the krewe gathers, plans adventures, raises a glass, and keeps the pirate spirit alive between events.

Community First

Pirates Giving Back

Pirates of the White Sands is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting local charities and our community.

Throughout the year, our krewe volunteers, raises funds, makes appearances, and lends a hand to organizations doing good in our area, including programs such as Toys for Kids and the Miracle League.
