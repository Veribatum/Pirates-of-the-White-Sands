Who We ARRR!

Pirates of the White Sands is a nonprofit pirate krewe based in Panama City, Florida.

We bring together costuming, community events, charity work, living history, and a shared love of pirate culture.

You’ll find us supporting local causes, appearing at festivals and special events, and helping bring a little adventure to the Gulf Coast.

We are a Krewe of Pirates that has run ashore to give back

From pirate festivals and parades to charity events and community appearances, the krewe stays busy throughout the year.

Pirates of the High Seas & Renaissance Fest

Each year, Pirates of the White Sands helps bring pirate history to life during Panama City Beach’s Pirates of the High Seas festival.

Our krewe creates a historical encampment and brings sword fighting, living history, educational demonstrations, pirate entertainment, and our ship float to the weekend.

Enjoy the Mayhem

Three Days of Pirate Mayhem

Pirates of the White Sands doesn’t just set up camp — we take the mayhem into the streets.

During Pirates of the High Seas, the krewe joins the parade aboard our pirate ship float while thousands of visitors line the route through Panama City Beach.

It’s one of the biggest weekends of the year for the crew.

Pirates Giving Back

Pirates of the White Sands is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting local charities and community programs.

Throughout the year, our krewe volunteers, raises funds, makes appearances, and lends a hand to organizations doing good in our area, including Toys for Kids and the Miracle League.

Our Home Port: Grace O’Malley’s

Every good krewe needs a home port.

For Pirates of the White Sands, that place is Grace O’Malley’s in Panama City.

Through the friendship between Captain Ransom and owner Marco Sparrow, Grace O’Malley’s has become a gathering place for the crew — a place to meet, plan adventures, raise a glass, and keep the pirate spirit alive between events.
