Who We ARRR!

Pirates of the White Sands is a nonprofit pirate crew based in Panama City, Florida. We bring together costuming, community events, charity work, and a shared love of pirate culture.

You’ll find us supporting local causes, appearing at festivals and special events, and helping bring a little adventure to the Gulf Coast.

Whether we’re raising funds, entertaining families, or simply showing up in full pirate regalia, the goal is the same: have fun, help the community, and leave things better than we found them.

Pirates Giving Back

Pirates of the White Sands is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting local charities and community programs.

Throughout the year, our krewe volunteers, raises funds, makes appearances, and lends a hand to organizations doing good in our area, including Toys for Kids and the Miracle League.

Our Home Port: Grace O’Malley’s

Every good krewe needs a home port.

For Pirates of the White Sands, that place is Grace O’Malley’s in Panama City.

Thanks to the friendship between Captain Ransom and owner Marco Sparrow, the pub has become a gathering place for the krewe — a place to meet, plan adventures, raise a glass, and occasionally cause a little pirate mayhem.

If you’re looking to cross paths with the crew ashore, Grace O’Malley’s is a good place to start.
