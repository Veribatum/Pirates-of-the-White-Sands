Who We ARRR!

Pirates of the White Sands is a nonprofit pirate krewe based in Panama City, Florida. We bring history, character, entertainment, and community spirit to local events while supporting charitable causes throughout the area.

Pirates of the High Seas

Each year, Pirates of the White Sands helps bring pirate history to life during Panama City Beach's Pirates of the High Seas festival.

Our krewe presents a historical encampment, sword-fighting demonstrations, living history, educational displays, and joins the parade aboard our pirate ship float.

Our Home Port

Every good krewe needs a home port. For Pirates of the White Sands, that place is Grace O'Malley's in Panama City.

It's where the crew gathers, plans adventures, raises a glass, and keeps the pirate spirit alive between events.

Pirates Giving Back

Pirates of the White Sands is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting local charities and community programs. Throughout the year, our krewe volunteers, raises funds, makes appearances, and lends a hand to organizations doing good in our area, including Toys for Kids and the Miracle League.
