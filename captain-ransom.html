Pirates of the White Sands

Captain Ransom

Captain Ransom King Mayhem • Captain & Crew Leader

Captain Ransom, Del McRea, is the founder and captain behind Pirates of the White Sands. His path into piracy began with a Captain Hook costume for a school event; what started as a character quickly grew into a lasting love of pirate history, authentic garb, and bringing that experience to the community.

He later built a local crew in Panama City, naming it for the white-sand beaches of the region. What began with only a couple of pirates grew into a much larger crew centered on historical presentation, family-friendly appearances, charitable work, and showing up for people who could use a little pirate mayhem in their day.

Under Captain Ransom, the crew has supported local causes, special-needs events, Toys for Kids, beach cleanups, community festivals, and Pirates of the High Seas. The mission behind the beard, eyepatch, and cannon smoke is simple: have fun, help people, and leave the community better than you found it.
